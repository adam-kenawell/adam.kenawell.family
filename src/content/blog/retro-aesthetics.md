## The appeal of retro

There's a reason pixel art and retro aesthetics keep coming back. They're nostalgic, distinctive, and — when done right — surprisingly elegant.

## Fusion Pixel

The font I'm using is **Fusion Pixel 12px Monospaced KR** from Fontsource. It's a bitmap-style font that gives everything a distinctly retro feel without sacrificing readability.

## Constraints breed creativity

Working with a pixel font means you can't rely on fancy typography tricks. Every element has to earn its place. That constraint pushes you toward cleaner, more intentional design.

## The color palette

Black and yellow is high contrast, bold, and a little unusual for a personal site. That's exactly why I like it. It stands out without being obnoxious.

# Hello World

Welcome to my corner of the internet. My name is Adam Kenawell, and this is my personal website.

I built this site as a place to share my thoughts, document projects I'm working on, and have a little fun with web development along the way.

## Why a personal site?

In an age of social media, there's something satisfying about owning your own space on the web. No algorithms, no feeds — just a simple page that I control.

## What to expect

I plan to write about:

- **Software engineering** — things I'm learning, tools I'm using, problems I've solved
- **Projects** — deep dives into side projects and experiments
- **Misc** — anything else that catches my interest

Stay tuned. There's more to come.

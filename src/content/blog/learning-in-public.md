## The idea

"Learning in public" is a concept I picked up from the dev community. Instead of learning quietly and hoping to remember things, you write about it. You share it. You make it real.

## Why it works

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain something in a blog post, I probably don't understand it well enough. The act of writing is the act of learning.

## The fear

Putting your thoughts out there is uncomfortable. What if I'm wrong? What if someone knows more? But that's kind of the point — being wrong publicly means you get corrected faster.

## The commitment

This blog is my commitment to learning in public. Some posts will be polished. Some will be rough. All of them will be honest.

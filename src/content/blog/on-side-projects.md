## The problem

Every developer knows the feeling. You start a side project full of energy, get 70% of the way there, then... a new shiny idea appears. The old project gathers dust.

## My graveyard

I've got repos that haven't been touched in years. Half-built apps, abandoned experiments, ideas that seemed brilliant at 2am. Sound familiar?

## The fix (maybe)

This site is my attempt to break the cycle. It's small, scoped, and public. Having something live on the internet creates just enough accountability to keep going.

## The lesson

Ship small. Ship often. A finished project that's 80% of your vision is infinitely more valuable than a perfect project that only exists in your head.
